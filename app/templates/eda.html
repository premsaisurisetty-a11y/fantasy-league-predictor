Key EDA Findings & Modeling Takeaways

Empirical evidence guiding LightGBM feature selection

01
Form Autocorrelation Dominates:

recent_average_points ($r=0.63$) and player_form ($r=0.58$) are the highest predictors, proving that short-term form heavily drives next-match output.

02
Wickets Yield Asymmetric Returns:

bowling_wickets ($r=0.50$) correlates twice as strongly as raw batting_runs ($r=0.27$) due to high discrete 25-point wicket rewards.

03
All-Rounders Hold Highest Point Ceilings:

All-Rounders produce an average of 44.8 points (vs 28.9 for Bowlers) because they generate returns from both batting and bowling quotas.

04
Target Positive Skewness ($\gamma_1 = +0.42$):

The unimodal target distribution contains explosive match-winning scores ($>80$ pts). Gradient-boosted decision trees naturally handle these non-linear upper tails.
